**July 4th weekend** — America's 250th birthday, which means the city is in full mode: Grand Park's Summer Block Party (July 4), fireworks visible from most rooftops, FIFA World Cup Fan Zone running at Hansen Dam through July 5 (Dodgers vs Padres at the Stadium through the 5th too). Anime Expo takes over the Convention Center July 2–5 — expect the city's transit and rideshare to be congested eastside. Plan ahead if you're going to any club night on July 3–4.
