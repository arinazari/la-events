Fourth of July weekend eats the next few days — fireworks everywhere and two separate WORK
warehouse nights (Pan-Pot tonight into Robert Hood tomorrow) anchor it if you want the
anti-fireworks plan. Otherwise it's a quiet run into a real one: the Vista's got a run of
strong 70mm/rep bookings and Level 8's rooftop keeps doing its thing most weekends.

- **Fri 7/24 — [STRANGER THAN presents: Stavroz (Live)](https://ra.co/events/2437239)** — The Bridge, DTLA. Belgian live-electronic band in the organic/melodic Innervisions-adjacent lane — a live-band set is a real change of pace from the DJ-heavy run.

## Around town

- FIFA World Cup 26 group-stage matches are underway across LA through mid-July (SoFi Stadium + fan zones at Hansen Dam and the Hammer Museum) — expect traffic/crowd spillover near Inglewood and Westwood on match days.
- Fourth of July weekend blankets the whole city: fireworks/drone shows in nearly every neighborhood (Grand Park, Marina del Rey, Long Beach, Burbank, Woodland Hills, Torrance, Pacific Palisades) plus the Coliseum's America 250 concert and Hollywood Bowl fireworks nights — heavier-than-usual traffic and closures citywide July 3–5.
- Anime Expo runs through July 5 at the LA Convention Center, driving a wave of anime-themed afterparties/raves around downtown and Historic Filipinotown this weekend.
