# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sat 7/4 — picks updated.*

*Same lane as last cycle, just stretched across three weekends instead of one holiday weekend doing all the work: DTLA warehouse techno and rooftop/afters house still carry the calendar. Robert Hood is still the standout — same booking as last week, still unclaimed, still the best thing on the books. Good depth in the smaller-room stuff too (Servito/Kendig, the all-vinyl night at Information, Felix Da Housecat). Straight read, again: nothing this cycle lands in the SWANA / Habibeats corner — still the club-nights list, not the diaspora one.*

---

## Saturday · July 4

- **[WORK presents: Robert Hood, Disco Zombie, Ønyx, & Rubbermiro](https://ra.co/events/2434712)** — TBA, Los Angeles · $24 b4 12 / $35+
  Robert Hood in a TBA warehouse on July 4th is the anti-fireworks plan — stripped, relentless M-Plant minimal with a crew of actual LA heads supporting, and WORK's address-drop format keeps the room self-selecting.

- **[Jayda G](https://dice.fm/event/k6dyll-jayda-g-4th-jul-sound-nightclub-los-angeles-tickets)** — Sound Nightclub, Hollywood · $31
  Sound is a proper room for Jayda G — she thrives in longer, sweatier sets pulling the floor through disco into gospel-house without a hard stop. The pick if warehouse TBAs feel like too much admin tonight.

- **[Midnight Lovers with Eli Escobar (Day & Night)](https://ra.co/events/2415277)** — The Bridge, DTLA · $22 pre
  A day-into-night set from a genuine NYC disco-house veteran — warm, record-collection-deep rather than peak-time, a solid Fourth of July anchor if you don't want to chase a TBA address.

- **[Okayval, Silvertone, Xvirad, Jacz](https://posh.vip/e/okayval-la-headline-show-1?t=infohz)** — TBA, DTLA · free
  Free-entry techno/acid/house warehouse bill — two names still building their catalogs, not a must, but zero cost of entry if you're already out tonight.

## Friday · July 10

- **[Great Quality Entertainment presents Township Rebellion with Trou and DENYC](https://ra.co/events/2474681)** — 1720, Arts District · $21 pre
  Township Rebellion's melodic, emotionally-pitched techno — closer to Lane 8 than a warehouse hammer — at one of the better rooms in the Arts District; Trou's an unknown quantity on the bill.

- **[Cirra presents: Day Thief, Faited, and sosoftly](https://ra.co/events/2472986)** — TBA, DTLA · $20-50
  Faited runs sound for Eris Drew's crew — a genuinely rawer, SF/Oakland queer-underground flavor of afters instead of the usual DTLA regulars.

## Saturday · July 11

- **[SMD - Mike Servito & Jason Kendig](https://ra.co/events/2477599)** — TBA, DTLA · $47 pre
  Servito and Kendig have years of Honcho and Honey Soundsystem warehouse nights together and know how to build a room slowly — the TBA address keeps it self-selecting, which fits the sound.

- **[Simon Says: Felix Da Housecat](https://dice.fm/event/oe73bg-simon-says-felix-da-housecat-11th-jul-the-spotlight-los-angeles-tickets)** — The Spotlight · $19+
  A genuine Chicago house/electroclash legend on a small-room bill — worth catching even on a random Friday if you're already near The Spotlight.

## Saturday · July 18

- **[Information - All Vinyl Night w/ Taylor Shockley + Luke Mele + CHOíCE](https://ra.co/events/2456585)** — TBA, DTLA · price TBA
  An all-vinyl night at a TBA Downtown space is the sweet spot on paper — Luke Mele's NTS-honed Balearic/ambient/dub sensibility, quieter and more textural than a peak-time club.
