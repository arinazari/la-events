# Ari's LA Events Digest
*Digest regenerated Sun 7/12 — picks updated.*

*Updated Sun 7/12 · Taste: house/techno/rooftop-vinyl-groove core, warehouse afterhours, rep cinema + small-room indie · Silver Lake*

---
Quiet before it's loud: the week ahead is mostly rep-cinema filler, but a rare Sunday-night Flying Lotus show tonight kicks things off right. From there the next three weekends build fast — Stephan Bodzin's live rig at the pier, Stavroz live and Boris Brejcha's orchestral techno set at the Bowl — before HARD Summer closes it all out August 1–2, with Kim Gordon playing the same Saturday as a heavier alternative to the fairgrounds.

## Top Picks

### Sunday · July 12
**[Flying Lotus](https://www.ticketweb.com/event/flying-lotus-with-live-band-blue-note-los-angeles-tickets/14908803)** — Blue Note, Hollywood · 9pm
Brainfeeder founder with a live band, not a DJ set — the jazzier, more improvisational side of his catalog in an unusually intimate room.

### Friday · July 17
**[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3745?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 10:30pm
Nolan's epic in true 70mm — a format event as much as a film one, exactly the reason this theater exists. Running most nights this stretch if Friday doesn't fit.

### Saturday · July 18
**[Stephan Bodzin (Live), SKALA, MANTi, Bibo, DENYC](https://ra.co/events/2423110)** — Santa Monica Pier · 3pm–10:30pm
Full live hardware rig from a genuine melodic-techno headliner (Afterlife/Awakenings tier) — oceanfront into the evening, an odd but fun setting for it.

### Friday · July 24
**[STRANGER THAN presents: Stavroz (Live)](https://ra.co/events/2437239)** — The Bridge, DTLA · 10pm–3am
Belgian live band, not DJs — warmer and more cinematic than another club night, closer to the Bonobo/RÜFÜS Du Sol lane.

### Sunday · July 26
**[Boris Brejcha with the Hollywood Bowl Orchestra](https://ra.co/events/2375675)** — Hollywood Bowl · 7pm
His first-ever pairing with a live orchestra — meticulous, melodic "high-tech minimal" techno is probably the one sound actually built for this room.

### Saturday · August 1
**[HARD Summer Music Festival](https://on.fgtix.com/trk/5oHm)** — Hollywood Park Grounds, Inglewood · Sat–Sun, 2pm
Charlotte de Witte, Amelie Lens, VTSS, Boys Noize and Vintage Culture give the techno stage real weight against a 60+ act bill — go for a handful of sets, not the whole grounds. Two-day ticket; Sunday's lineup (Amelie Lens, Vintage Culture again) holds up too.

**[Kim Gordon - Play Me Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/kim-gordon-play-me-tour-los-angeles-california-08-01-2026/event/09006482BE637FB6)** — Pacific Electric, DTLA · 8pm
Sonic Youth's Kim Gordon solo — weirder and more interesting than a legacy lap, with SASAMI opening. The indie/experimental alternative if the fairgrounds aren't calling.

**[Saturday HARDfest Afters (Mau P, Dreya V)](https://ra.co/events/2479700)** — TBA, Mission Four (Ace/Mission Studios) · 10pm
Real tech-house afters to close the night if you're staying out from the grounds — Mau P's raw, jacking sets are a genuine tracked-name booking, not just a support slot.
