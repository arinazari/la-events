# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sun 7/5 — picks updated.*

*Ari's next few weeks, ranked for you — a stacked deep-house day party, Stephan Bodzin and Boris Brejcha going big outdoors, a five-night Odyssey 70mm run at the Vista, and rep-cinema gems filling the gaps.*

## Sunday · July 5

- **[Day Trip In The Park LA: Hayden James, Gudfella, Local Singles](https://ra.co/events/2436973)** — Los Angeles State Historic Park, Chinatown · $37 pre
  Hayden James is genuine core rotation for you, and this daytime outdoor party fits the mainstream-polish lane better than "underground" — and it's today.

## Wednesday · July 8

- **[Rabit presented by VLSF & KCRW](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/090064BB0DF7BC3F)** — The Echo, Echo Park · $27
  A real PTP-orbit deconstructed-club/experimental producer, with KCRW + VLSF co-presenting as a serious curatorial signal — the underground, European-leaning booking this lane wants.

## Friday · July 10

- **[Hollywood 90028](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3815?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  A rediscovered cult grindhouse curio, recently restored by Grindhouse Releasing after decades unseen — exactly the deep-cut the Vista midnight slot exists for.
- **[Heat (1995)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/4789?siteToken=fmtswb0qqbym3de6c4bbsqj89m)** — New Beverly Cinema, Fairfax
  Michael Mann's Heat on a real screen — one of the great LA crime films, in an LA-set heist no less. Lead with this.
- **[The Wailing](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/the-wailing-7-10-26-315-pm/)** — Vidiots, Eagle Rock
  One of the great modern horror films (Na Hong-jin) — a standout even at a 3pm slot, don't let the daytime hour bury it.

## Saturday · July 11

- **[Certified Groovers x SNEAK'S BEATS: Louie Vega, Karizma, DJ Sneak, Heidi Lawden, Juliet Mendoza](https://ra.co/events/2402790)** — TBA warehouse, Los Angeles · $40
  Louie Vega, Karizma, and DJ Sneak on one bill is deep-house and garage-house royalty — this lineup is way better than the flat score suggests.
- **[I LOVE DNB: Jumpin' Jack Frost + Ray Keith](https://ra.co/events/2441821)** — TBA, Los Angeles · $23 pre
  Jumpin' Jack Frost and Ray Keith are foundational jungle/DNB figures — a genuine RA-listed underground booking, not the usual TBA noise.
- **[Lenny Kaye (Patti Smith Band), Cindy Lee Berryhill](https://dice.fm/event/mx98nl-lenny-kaye-patti-smith-band-cindy-lee-berryhill-11th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown
  Lenny Kaye — Patti Smith Group guitarist and the guy who compiled Nuggets, basically inventing the garage-punk canon — in an intimate Frogtown room. A rare one.

## Wednesday · July 15

- **[Eartheater](https://www.ticketweb.com/event/eartheater-the-masonic-lodge-at-hollywood-tickets/14915813)** — Masonic Lodge at Hollywood Forever, Hollywood · $14-39
  A genuine draw — PAN/Anticon-orbit experimental vocalist with real critical and festival cred — and the Masonic Lodge is a stunning room for it.

## Thursday · July 16

- **[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3743?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Christopher Nolan's Odyssey in true 70mm is a format event as much as a film one — the print-and-projector reason to go instead of streaming it. Also playing 7/17, 7/19, 7/21, and 7/23 if this date doesn't work.

## Friday · July 17

- **[Lights Down Low feat. Mura Masa](https://ra.co/events/2452948)** — TBA, DTLA
  Mura Masa headlining a DTLA afters is a real name-draw booking, though more pop-production than club-selector — go for the name, not deep selection.

## Saturday · July 18

- **[Stephan Bodzin (Live), SKALA, MANTi, Bibo, DENYC](https://ra.co/events/2423110)** — Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica · $51 b4 5 / $61-221
  Stephan Bodzin live is a real melodic-techno headliner (Afterlife/Awakenings tier), open-air on the pier into the evening — the score badly underrates this one.

## Friday · July 24

- **[STRANGER THAN presents: Stavroz (Live)](https://ra.co/events/2437239)** — The Bridge, DTLA
  Stavroz play live rather than DJ — closer to the Bonobo/RÜFÜS Du Sol live-electronic lane than a club night. Warm, cinematic, the melodic-organic end of the taste without the warehouse chase.

## Saturday · July 25

- **[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Jody Wisternoff [2-8 PM]](https://ra.co/events/2449781)** — Level 8 DTLA, DTLA
  A genuinely tasteful, long-running selector — the template daytime rooftop pool party, melodic progressive house from an actual Anjunadeep mainstay.
- **[CSW - Nick León [All Night]](https://ra.co/events/2475085)** — TBA, DTLA
  A legit rising name in the Latin/club-fusion space with real label cosigns — Miami dembow-and-dancehall rhythm woven into club music, one of the more interesting bookings in this batch.

## Sunday · July 26

- **[Boris Brejcha with the Hollywood Bowl Orchestra](https://ra.co/events/2375675)** — Hollywood Bowl, Hollywood
  His first-ever performance with a live orchestra, part of KCRW Festival at the Bowl — a genuinely rare booking the flat score badly undersells.
