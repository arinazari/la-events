Three weekends out, the standout is Beltran's open-to-close at Naud St — a proper FISHER/Chris Lake/Michael Bibi-tier tech-house night, Sunday of this week. The Vista's running Nolan's *Odyssey* in true 70mm across nearly every remaining date this month (pick whichever night fits), Stephan Bodzin brings live melodic techno to Santa Monica Pier, and Boris Brejcha's doing his first-ever orchestral set at the Bowl closing out the run. Full sprawl is in the consolidated digest; this is the cut worth actually planning around.
