# Ari's LA Events Digest
*Updated Tue 7/7 · Taste: house/techno/rooftop-vinyl-groove core, warehouse afterhours, rep cinema + small-room indie · Silver Lake*

---
Three weekends out, the standouts skew rooftop and live-electronic over straight club nights — Sunset Sessions is back at Level 8, Stavroz and Bob Moses bring the Bonobo/RÜFÜS live lane, and the Vista's running Nolan's *Odyssey* in 70mm most of the run. Full sprawl (every screening, every TBA afters) is in the consolidated digest; this is the cut worth actually planning around.

## Top Picks

### Friday · July 10
**[SUNSET SESSIONS](https://ra.co/events/2484237)** — Level 8 DTLA · 5pm
The actual rooftop-golden-hour-house night your taste keeps citing as the reference point — Tamara Lanza back on the decks. If you only do one thing this weekend, make it this.

**[Lights Down Low feat. Uffie x Mia Moretti](https://ra.co/events/2484126)** — TBA, Silver Lake · 11:59pm
Bloghouse nostalgia done right — Uffie hasn't played an LA lineup in years, and it's walkable. Fun one-off, not the deep-groove lane, but a real booking.

### Saturday · July 11
**[Certified Groovers x SNEAK'S BEATS](https://ra.co/events/2402790)** — TBA warehouse, DTLA · 2pm-midnight · $40
Louie Vega, Karizma, and DJ Sneak on one bill is deep-house and garage-house royalty — a flat score badly undersells this one.

**[Lenny Kaye (Patti Smith Band), Cindy Lee Berryhill](https://dice.fm/event/mx98nl-lenny-kaye-patti-smith-band-cindy-lee-berryhill-11th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7pm
The guy who compiled *Nuggets* and played guitar behind Patti Smith, in an intimate Frogtown room. Rare.

### Sunday · July 12
**[Sunday Sessions LA (Vinyl Only) Open Air](https://ra.co/events/2478840)** — Apotheke, Chinatown · 6pm · free b4 7pm
All-vinyl Sunday open-air, dead center of the rooftop/groove lane even without marquee names.

### Friday · July 17
**[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3745?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 10:30pm
Nolan's *Odyssey* in true 70mm — a format event as much as a film one, and the reason this venue exists. Also running Thu 7/16, Sun 7/19, and a few more dates this week.

### Saturday · July 18
**[Stephan Bodzin (Live), SKALA, MANTi, Bibo](https://ra.co/events/2423110)** — Santa Monica Pier · 3pm-10:30pm · $51 b4 5 / $61-221
Afterlife/Awakenings-tier melodic techno, live, open-air on the pier into the evening. The score undersells this hard.

**[DUBLAB presents: BAE BAE, DJ Noir, Boo Lean, Spiñorita](https://ra.co/events/2482750)** — The Airliner, Lincoln Heights · 10pm
Four deep, genre-blurring LA vinyl-diggers on a dublab-curated bill at a scrappy bar — the eclectic-groove night this lane exists for, and close to home.

### Sunday · July 19
**[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3747?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 7pm
Same run, one night later — still the standout theatrical booking of the month.

**[Bob Moses and Cannons: Afterglow Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7-Obt)** — Hollywood Bowl · 7pm · $18+
Legit live-electronic act (Rüfüs/ODESZA tier) at a venue worth building a night around.

### Friday · July 24
**[STRANGER THAN presents: Stavroz (Live)](https://ra.co/events/2437239)** — The Bridge, DTLA · 10pm
Belgian live band in the organic/melodic Bonobo-RÜFÜS lane — warmer and more cinematic than another DJ night, and a rarer booking for it.

**[Fight Without Jingi](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3850?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 8pm
Kinji Fukasaku's *Battles Without Honor and Humanity* under its literal title — a genuinely rare yakuza-genre import, bracing over comfort-rewatch.

### Saturday · July 25
**[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Jody Wisternoff](https://ra.co/events/2449781)** — Level 8 DTLA · 2pm-8pm
Anjunadeep's own Way Out West half doing daytime melodic progressive house on a rooftop — the template. Build the Saturday around it.

**[CSW - Nick León [All Night]](https://ra.co/events/2475085)** — TBA, DTLA · 10pm
Miami dembow-and-club fusion from one of the more genuinely distinctive producers going right now — not a straight house night, which is the point.

### Sunday · July 26
**[Boris Brejcha with the Hollywood Bowl Orchestra](https://ra.co/events/2375675)** — Hollywood Bowl · 7pm
First-ever Brejcha-with-orchestra pairing, part of KCRW Festival at a venue you love. A genuinely rare booking a flat score-4 badly undersells.
