## This Weekend (Fri–Sat 6/26–6/27)

Friday 6/26 is an unusually stacked night — several things worth deciding between.

⭐ **Lights Down Low feat. Yaeji** — TBA Downtown LA · 10pm · $22–47
Yaeji is a genuine draw: her hybrid of house and left-field pop is rare at this kind of night. A tracked artist at a proper TBA room; build the evening around this one.

⭐ **Dirty Epic & INCOGNITO present: Stacey Pullen, BAE BAE** — TBA · 11pm · $27/$32
Detroit techno royalty — Stacey Pullen is a foundational name (Underground Resistance orbit) booked by Dirty Epic, one of the most trusted local promoters. The late start suits a post-dinner pickup.

**Sunset Sessions 'Unicorns-Only' Takeover** ✓ — Level 8 DTLA · 5pm · free
The recurring rooftop-house afternoon on the Level 8 terrace. Free, outdoor, groove-forward — good pre-dinner or early-evening move before the later TBA nights.

**Midnight Lovers with JKriv (Razor-N-Tape)** ✓ — Bar Franca · 9pm · free
JKriv founded Razor-N-Tape, one of the sharpest disco/edit labels around. Free at a cocktail bar — easy add-on before or after a late night.

**Green Velvet & Ugo B at Exchange LA** ✓ — Day Trip SoCal Prep Party · 10pm · $45–95
Green Velvet (Relief Records, Chicago house/techno) headlining the official Day Trip preparty. More festival-adjacent energy than the TBA warehouse nights — good if the bigger room suits better.

## 4th of July Weekend (7/3–7/4)

⭐ **WORK presents: Pan-Pot [4 Hour Set], DOOSTAH, Kitty Logic** — TBA Los Angeles · 11pm · TBA
Pan-Pot are a Berlin techno institution and they're going four hours at a TBA warehouse. That format — open-to-close, proper room, serious booking — is rare in LA. Mark this one.

⭐ **WORK presents: Robert Hood, Disco Zombie, Ønyx, Rubbermiro** — TBA Los Angeles · 11pm · TBA
Robert Hood is Underground Resistance and one of the founding names in Detroit techno. WORK booking him on the 4th is the headline of the weekend.

**Midnight Lovers: Eli Escobar** ✓ — TBA DTLA house/disco · 7pm–2am · $22 pre
Eli Escobar is a legit NY selector with Razor-N-Tape credentials; Midnight Lovers is a reliable house/disco series. The 7pm start makes this an early option before the WORK late nights.

**Young Franco Pool Party at Level 8** ✓ — Level 8 DTLA · 1pm · $26
Breezy Aussie house in the afternoon on the Level 8 rooftop for the 4th. Not a serious DJ set, but a pool party in daylight — good warm-up energy if the holiday calls for it.

**Groove Room: A House and Hits Dance Party** — Gold Diggers, East Hollywood · 10:30pm · TBA *(7/3)*
Gold Diggers is a reliable mid-size room. Pre-holiday Thursday — serviceable if the Pan-Pot night fills.
