That said, the next three weekends split cleanly into two lanes that do line up with the stated taste: a strong run of Vista Theater rep cinema, and a cluster of DTLA rooftop/groove-house nights.

## Top Picks

### Fri · 7/17
**[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3745?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 10:30pm
Nolan's Homer adaptation on a true 70mm print — a format event as much as a film one, and exactly the reason to go to the Vista instead of streaming it later. Also running Sat and Sun if Friday doesn't work.

**[Low Cut Connie](https://www.troubadour.com/)** — Troubadour, West Hollywood · 7pm
Straightforward pick: a loved room (Troubadour), a reliably great live rock/soul act, nothing to overthink.

### Sat · 7/18
**[Fandango](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3845?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 8pm
Low-stakes and warm if you'd rather skip the club circuit: Costner's first lead role, 1985 cult favorite, five friends' last road trip before the draft.

**[Acts of Service Sounds](https://dice.fm/event/nv6wv8-acts-of-service-sounds-a-night-of-soulful-house-17th-jul-gold-diggers-los-angeles-tickets)** — Gold Diggers, East Hollywood · 10:30pm
A recurring soulful-house residency — reliable groove over a big-name lineup, and the kind of relaxed, intimate-room DJ set this profile actually likes over a warehouse peak-time night.

### Fri · 7/24
**[Fight Without Jingi](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3850?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 8pm
Kinji Fukasaku's "Battles Without Honor and Humanity" — the documentary-style, ultra-violent yakuza classic that basically invented its genre. Bracing rep-cinema pick instead of another comfort watch.

**[Trainspotting](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/trainspotting-7-24-26-415-pm/)** — Vidiots, Eagle Rock · 4:15pm
Danny Boyle's 1996 classic on a real screen — worth it even on a rewatch.

### Sat · 7/25
**[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Jody Wisternoff](https://ra.co/events/2449781)** — Level 8, DTLA · 2pm–8pm
The template pick of this batch: daytime rooftop, melodic progressive house from an actual Anjunadeep mainstay. Walkable from DTLA, outdoor setting — hits three boosts at once.

**[Certified Groovers: ARTUR & GILBERTO](https://ra.co/events/2488322)** — TBA, Downtown LA · 11pm
All-night groove-house set, close to home if FLOAT runs late into the evening and you want a second stop.

### Fri · 7/31
**[SEBASTIANE](https://ra.co/events/2471583)** — Akbar, Silver Lake · 10pm
Silver Lake's tiny, unpretentious queer neighborhood bar rather than a big production — an easy, close-to-home dive-bar dancefloor.

**[12 Angry Men](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/sessions?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 8pm
Sidney Lumet's jury-room classic — tight, tense, hard to go wrong with at the Vista.

### Sat · 8/1
**[Deadhead Disco: Jerry Garcia's Birthday Celebration](https://www.ticketmaster.com/deadhead-disco-jerry-garcias-birthday-celebration-los-angeles-california-08-01-2026/event/090064A5B278E816)** — Teragram Ballroom, Westlake · 9pm
Teragram is one of the named-loved venues on this profile, and a Garcia-birthday disco set is a fun excuse to end up there.

**[Certified Groovers with Demuir](https://ra.co/events/2464585)** — TBA, Downtown LA · 10pm
Demuir basically named the "jackin' house" subgenre — funky, groove-first Chicago house, a stronger pick than most of the generic "electronic, TBA" listings around it.

**[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Chambord](https://ra.co/events/2449786)** — Level 8, DTLA · 2pm
Same rooftop-pool formula as the Wisternoff date a week earlier; Chambord's a local eclectic-melodic booking rather than a marquee name, but the setting alone earns a look.
