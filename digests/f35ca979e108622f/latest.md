## Weekend of Fri 7/17 – Sun 7/19 (plus Thu 7/16)

- **Thu 7/16 + Fri 7/17 · [Yoshiki — Scarlet Night / Violet Night](https://www.musiccenter.org/tickets-free-events/lease-events/yoshiki/)** — Walt Disney Concert Hall, 8pm both nights — two different programs from the X Japan founder doing his rock/classical-crossover thing at a venue you already love. Go Friday if you're only picking one — it also catches the Friday-night boost.
- **Fri 7/17 · [The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3745?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz, 10:30pm — Nolan's new epic in true 70mm, the exact reason the Vista's projector exists. Late enough to stack after Yoshiki if you're ambitious.
- **Fri 7/17 · [Perfect Blue](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/perfect-blue-7-17-26-945-pm/)** or **[All That Jazz](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/all-that-jazz-7-17-26-400-pm/)** — Vidiots, Eagle Rock — a strong double option if you'd rather do a rep-cinema night instead: Satoshi Kon's psych-thriller anime at 9:45pm, or Fosse's "All That Jazz" earlier at 4pm.
- **Sat 7/18 · [The Thing](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/the-thing-on-35mm-7-18-26-945-pm/)** (Vidiots, 35mm, 9:45pm) or **[A Clockwork Orange](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/4806?siteToken=fmtswb0qqbym3de6c4bbsqj89m)** (New Beverly, 7:30pm) — pick your Saturday-night genre poison.
- **Sun 7/19 · [Bob Moses and Cannons: Afterglow Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7-Obt)** — Hollywood Bowl, 7pm, $18+ — melodic, atmospheric electronic-pop at a big outdoor venue is precisely your "progressive EDM at large venues" high-interest category; a good way to close the weekend.

## Weekend of Fri 7/24 – Sun 7/26

- **Fri 7/24 · [Fight Without Jingi](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3850?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, 8pm — this is Kinji Fukasaku's "Battles Without Honor and Humanity" under its literal title: the documentary-style, ultra-violent yakuza classic that basically invented the genre. The best rep-cinema pick of the whole three weeks if you want something bracing instead of another comfort-watch.
- **Sat 7/25 · [Dazed and Confused](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/4829?siteToken=fmtswb0qqbym3de6c4bbsqj89m)** — New Beverly Cinema, 11:59pm — late-night nostalgia rep, low-stakes and easy.
- **Sun 7/26 · [Boris Brejcha with the Hollywood Bowl Orchestra](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7x7ap)** — Hollywood Bowl, 7pm — a genuine marquee-name electronic artist paired with a full orchestra outdoors; closes the stretch on the same "big-venue melodic electronic" note Bob Moses opened it on.
