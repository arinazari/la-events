## This Weekend (Fri 6/26 – Sat 6/27)

Friday is unusually stacked — several things worth deciding between, some overlapping.

⭐ **Lights Down Low feat. Yaeji** — TBA Downtown LA · 10pm · $22–47
Yaeji is a genuine draw: her hybrid of house and left-field pop is rare at this kind of night. The kind of booking Lights Down Low earns its rep on. Build the evening around this one. [Tickets](https://ra.co/events/2443844)

⭐ **Dirty Epic & INCOGNITO: Stacey Pullen, BAE BAE** — TBA · 11pm · $27 before midnight / $32
Stacey Pullen is Detroit techno royalty (Underground Resistance orbit) booked by Dirty Epic, one of the most trusted underground promoters in LA. RA pick. Goes late. [Tickets](https://ra.co/events/2415209)

**★ Day Trip SoCal Prep Party: Green Velvet & Ugo Banchi** — Exchange LA · 10pm · $45–95
Green Velvet (Relief Records, Chicago house/techno) headlining the official Day Trip preparty. More festival-adjacent energy than the TBA warehouse nights — a real booking, not a warm-up slot. [Tickets](https://ra.co/events/2455039)

**★ Midnight Lovers with JKriv (Razor-N-Tape)** — Bar Franca, Silver Lake · 9pm · free
JKriv co-founded Razor-N-Tape, one of the sharpest disco/edit labels going. Free at a cocktail bar — easy add-on before heading somewhere late. [Details](https://ra.co/events/2396500)

**★ Flying Mojito Bros, Brainstory** — Camille's at The Bellwether · $24
Desert house/disco on one bill, Brainstory bringing the live soul angle. Bellwether is the right room for this energy — a proper alternative to the TBA warehouse circuit. [Tickets](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXPmtFPj3pL/)

**Sunset Sessions 'Unicorns-Only' Takeover** — Level 8 DTLA · 5pm · free
Recurring rooftop-house afternoon on the Level 8 terrace. Free, outdoor, golden hour — good early move before the later nights. [Details](https://ra.co/events/2452837)

## 4th of July Weekend (7/3–7/5)

⭐ **WORK presents: Pan-Pot [4 Hour Set], DOOSTAH & Kitty Logic** — TBA Los Angeles · 11pm *(Fri 7/3)*
Pan-Pot are a Berlin techno institution going four hours at a TBA warehouse — open-format, proper room, serious booking. That combination is rare in LA. RA pick. [Tickets](https://ra.co/events/2414397)

⭐ **WORK presents: Robert Hood, Disco Zombie, Ønyx & Rubbermiro** — TBA Los Angeles · 11pm *(Sat 7/4)*
Robert Hood is Underground Resistance and one of the founding names in Detroit techno. WORK on the 4th — the serious option for the weekend. [Tickets](https://ra.co/events/2434712)

**★ Midnight Lovers: Eli Escobar** — TBA DTLA house/disco · 7pm–2am · $22 pre *(Sat 7/4)*
Eli Escobar has a deep NY house/disco discography and a selector's ear. Midnight Lovers is a reliable series; the early start makes this a clean option before the WORK late night. [Tickets](https://ra.co/events/2415277)

**★ Young Franco Pool Party at Level 8** — Level 8 DTLA · 1pm · $26 *(Sat 7/4)*
Breezy Australian melodic house on the rooftop for the 4th. Closer to your melodic EDM lane than the warehouse nights — good daylight option if the holiday calls for something lighter. [Details](https://ra.co/events/2449889)

**Ginger Root at Camille's** — The Bellwether · free *(Fri 7/3)*
Cameron Lew's indie-funk project. Free show at a great room — easy, low-key start to the holiday weekend. [Details](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZnMuYqjFAC/)
