# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sat 7/4 — picks updated.*

*This cycle has real range for her taste: Robert Hood — one of her tracked names — headlining a Fourth-of-July warehouse, Yoshiki bringing something genuinely different to Walt Disney Concert Hall (a room she already loves), and an all-vinyl listening-bar night built for sitting and listening rather than peak-time drops. The rest of the pool is still thick with unnamed TBA techno and hard-techno warehouse filler that doesn't clear the bar here — the picks below lean instead on her actual high categories: rep cinema at the Vista, a disco/groove night, a food night market, and marquee melodic-EDM at a proper venue when it shows up.*
