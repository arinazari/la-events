# Dr. Ganesan's LA Events Digest
*Digest regenerated Wed 7/15 — picks updated.*

*Next three weekends, ranked to your profile: intimate live music, rep cinema, food/drink
pop-ups, melodic/progressive EDM, standup + theater, daytime markets, and groove-not-grind
house. DTLA's having a huge run of underground techno/hard-house raves this whole stretch —
I've filtered most of it out, since per your taste notes that's only worth it for marquee
names.*

## Weekend of Fri 7/17 – Sun 7/19

**Friday 7/17**
- **["Starman" (8pm) + "The Odyssey" 70mm (10:30pm) — double bill, Vista Theater](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/sessions?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)**, Los Feliz. Carpenter's gentle, warm 1984 sci-fi romance with Jeff Bridges, then Nolan's *Odyssey* in actual 70mm right after — a real print-and-projector reason to leave the house rather than stream it. Your venue, your lane.
- **[Yoshiki – Violet Night](https://www.musiccenter.org/tickets-free-events/lease-events/yoshiki/)**, Walt Disney Concert Hall, DTLA, 8pm. A genuine outlier: X Japan's Yoshiki doing orchestral, classical-crossover solo material in one of LA's best rooms. Not your usual lane at all, but a rare booking worth knowing about.

**Saturday 7/18**
- **[Fandango](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3845?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)**, Vista Theater, 8pm. Kevin Reynolds' 1985 cult favorite — Kevin Costner's first lead role, five Texas college friends on a last road trip before the draft. Warm, funny, unpretentious; one of the best rep picks on the whole slate this cycle.
- **[Higher Grooves Rooftop Party](https://ra.co/events/2489201)**, The Broken Shaker, DTLA, 7pm. Early-evening rooftop set from LA's own Made to Move crew (Akumen + agraybé) — the scrappier, local version of the sunset-groove thing, exactly the relaxed-house register you like.
- **[Los Lobos (free outdoor show)](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/the-best-things-to-do-in-la-this-weekend)**, Burton Chace Park, Marina Del Rey. Free, editorial-flagged, a solid low-key daytime-into-evening option if you're on the westside.
- Also around: **[The Hustle](https://dice.fm/event/l8xylp-the-hustle-first-and-third-saturday-disco-party-18th-jul-gold-diggers-los-angeles-tickets)** disco party at Gold Diggers (East Hollywood) — the reliable no-headliner boogie/classic-house residency, an easy default if nothing else is pulling you.

**Sunday 7/19**
- **[Sunday Sessions LA (Vinyl Only) Open Air](https://ra.co/events/2478841)**, 450 S Bixel St, DTLA, 6pm–12am. Free before 7pm; steady vinyl-house residents Hazy and Krane, not marquee names but reliably groove-first — the "relaxed house in a relaxed setting" box, checked.
- **[A Goofy Movie](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/4809?siteToken=fmtswb0qqbym3de6c4bbsqj89m)**, New Beverly Cinema, 2pm. Easy, nostalgic Sunday-matinee rep pick if you want something lighter than another courtroom drama or yakuza classic.

## Weekend of Fri 7/24 – Sun 7/26

**Friday 7/24**
- **["Fight Without Jingi" (a.k.a. Battles Without Honor and Humanity)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3850?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)**, Vista Theater, 8pm. Kinji Fukasaku's documentary-style, ultra-violent yakuza classic — basically invented the genre. If you want something bracing instead of a comfort rewatch, this is the rep pick of the whole cycle.
- **[STRANGER THAN presents Stavroz (Live)](https://ra.co/events/2437239)**, The Bridge, DTLA, 10pm. Belgian four-piece playing live (not DJing) — organic, cinematic electronic in the RÜFÜS DU SOL / Bonobo lane, built for a room that wants to feel rather than peak. The melodic-organic side of your taste without the warehouse chase.

**Saturday 7/25**
- **[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Jody Wisternoff](https://ra.co/events/2449781)**, Level 8 DTLA, 2–8pm. Build the day around this one: Wisternoff is one half of Way Out West and co-curates Anjunadeep's flagship mix series — deep, melodic progressive house at an actual rooftop pool, exactly the template your profile is chasing.
- **[Tawanda – Jazz & Soul with a Twist](https://www.vibratogrilljazz.com/music/tawanda-jazz-soul-with-a-twist-5)**, Vibrato Grill Jazz, Bel Air. If you want something more intimate and seated after the rooftop, an award-winning vocalist doing soulful jazz standards in a proper listening room.
- "Christmas in July" is also on at the Vista that night, but the actual film title hasn't posted yet — worth a peek at the listing before committing.

**Sunday 7/26**
- The Odyssey (70mm) is still running nightly at the Vista if you haven't caught it yet — otherwise a quiet Sunday on this slate; Vidiots has a run of older repertory titles if you want to browse in person.

## Weekend of Fri 7/31 – Sun 8/2

**Friday 7/31**
- **[12 Angry Men](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/sessions?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)**, Vista Theater, 8pm. Sidney Lumet's jury-room classic on the big screen — tight, tense, hard to go wrong with. A genuine credit to a rep-cinema habit.
- **[Brian Henson's Puppet Up!](https://www.ticketmaster.com/brian-hensons-puppet-up-hollywood-california-07-31-2026/event/0B0064A7A96D62FA)**, Ricardo Montalban Theatre, Hollywood, 3pm (16+; also an 8/1 7pm show). Fully improvised, adult-leaning puppet comedy from Jim Henson's son — the standup/theater crossover your profile is built for.

**Saturday 8/1**
- **[Certified Groovers with Demuir](https://ra.co/events/2464585)**, TBA – Downtown LA, 10pm. Toronto-via-Chicago producer Demuir basically created the "jackin' house" subgenre — funky, sexy, groove-first house, not a generic TBA listing. One of the better electronic bookings across all three weekends.
- **[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Chambord](https://ra.co/events/2449786)**, Level 8 DTLA, 2–8pm. Same rooftop-pool formula as the Wisternoff date a week earlier; Chambord's a local, eclectic-melodic duo rather than a marquee name, but the setting alone earns a look.

**Sunday 8/2**
- **[Death Cab for Cutie: I Built You A Tower World Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/death-cab-for-cutie-i-built-los-angeles-california-08-02-2026/event/09006435E6A2D26C)**, Greek Theatre, Los Feliz, 7:30pm, w/ Nation of Language. Right at one of your loved venues — a proper indie-rock headliner on an amphitheater stage, the best "big show, still feels intimate" pick of the whole stretch.
- **[Sunday Sessions LA (Vinyl Only) Open Air](https://ra.co/events/2482685)**, The Bridge, DTLA, 6pm. The vinyl-only series is back with Krane again — a genuine community, not a one-off booking, and the same relaxed-groove register as the 7/19 edition.
