# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sun 7/5 — picks updated.*

*Three weekends out, and the lane keeps showing up in the same two places — DTLA listening-bar/warehouse afters and a genuinely strong run of rep cinema at the Vista — with one clear daytime pick (a rooftop pool party with an Anjunadeep mainstay) worth building a Saturday around.*

## Friday · July 10

- **[Township Rebellion, Trou, DENYC](https://ra.co/events/2474681)** — 1720, Arts District · $21 pre
  Township Rebellion is the real draw — melodic, emotionally-pitched techno closer to Lane 8 than a warehouse hammer; go in expecting build and atmosphere over peak-time.
- **[Cirra presents: Day Thief, Faited, and sosoftly](https://ra.co/events/2472986)** — TBA, Downtown LA · $20-50
  Two names straight out of the SF/Oakland queer-underground soundsystem scene rather than the usual DTLA circuit regulars — a rawer flavor of afters.
- **[Recollect Underground: LA Riots, Beast, Jacz, Lavenge, Max Rush](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZl9KXzKRBX/)** — Los Globos, Silver Lake · $10 pre
  Solid afterhours option if you're already eastside, though the support slots are thin enough to read as good-not-essential.

## Saturday · July 11

- **[Chopper](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3839?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Eric Bana as real-life Melbourne gangland figure Mark "Chopper" Read — pitch-black Aussie crime-comedy that made Bana's career before Hollywood.
- **[Dances With Wolves](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3808?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Kevin Costner's epic, the 7:30 showing — pair it with Chopper for a Los Feliz double feature.

## Friday · July 17

- **[Lights Down Low feat. Mura Masa](https://ra.co/events/2452948)** — TBA, Downtown LA
  More pop-production than club-selector, but Lights Down Low booking him signals something interesting — go for the name recognition, not deep selection.
- **[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3745?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Christopher Nolan's film in true 70mm — a format event as much as a film one, exactly the print-and-projector reason to go instead of streaming it.

## Saturday · July 18

- **[Information - All Vinyl Night w/ Taylor Shockley + Luke Mele + CHOíCE](https://ra.co/events/2456585)** — TBA, Downtown LA
  Taylor Shockley is a respected LA selector building around Luke Mele's NTS-honed Balearic/ambient/dub sensibility — squarely the listening-bar format worth chasing an address for.
- **[Fandango](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3845?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Kevin Reynolds' 1985 cult favorite, Costner's first lead role — a low-stakes, unpretentious pick if you want warm and funny rather than heavy.

## Friday · July 24

- **[Fight Without Jingi](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3850?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Kinji Fukasaku's "Battles Without Honor and Humanity" — the documentary-style, ultra-violent yakuza classic that basically invented the jitsuroku genre. Bracing instead of another comfort rep-watch.

## Saturday · July 25

- **[FLOAT Rooftop Pool Party ft. Jody Wisternoff [2-8 PM]](https://ra.co/events/2449781)** — Level 8, DTLA
  The template: daytime rooftop pool party, melodic progressive house from an actual Anjunadeep mainstay. Build the Saturday around it.
- **[CSW - Nick León [All Night]](https://ra.co/events/2475085)** — TBA, Downtown
  Not a straight house night — Miami dembow-and-dancehall rhythm woven into club music, one of the more interesting bookings in this batch.
