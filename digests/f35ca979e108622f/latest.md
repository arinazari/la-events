# LA Events

*Digest regenerated Thu 7/2 — picks updated.*

*Three weeks out and the algorithm wants to hand you a wall of TBA warehouse techno — skip
most of it. The real strength this cycle is the rep-cinema bench at the Vista and New
Beverly, a couple of loved-venue live shows, and one big-room booking (Bob Moses at the
Bowl) that's a genuine hit for the melodic-EDM lane. Marc Maron's doing two nights at the
Elysian if you want comedy in the mix.*

## Friday · July 3

- **[Dirtybird x FLOAT: Road to Campout — Rooftop Pool Party](https://ra.co/events/2449768)** — Level 8 DTLA, DTLA · $30 pre
  Daytime, bouncy West Coast house on a DTLA rooftop (2-8pm) with Sacha Robotti and Ardalan, both real Dirtybird pedigree — the sun-and-speakers version of the relaxed-groove lane.
- **[Ginger Root (DJ Set)](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZnMuYqjFAC/?img_index=1)** — Camille's at The Bellwether, DTLA · free
  Indie/alt-disco musician spinning his own influences rather than performing — free, low-commitment, a nice change of pace from the club nights running the same night.
- **[Heat (1995)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/4774?siteToken=fmtswb0qqbym3de6c4bbsqj89m)** — New Beverly Cinema, Fairfax
  If you'd rather stay in than go out: Mann/De Niro/Pacino on 35mm at 7pm, hard to beat as a rep pick.

## Saturday · July 4

- **Fourth of July Fireworks Viewing at Mitsi** — Mitsi cocktail bar, Chinatown *(no ticket link — walk-in)*
  Art Deco rooftop bar, vinyl DJ, fireworks views — the outdoor/relaxed-setting pick for the holiday, and a lot less admin than a TBA warehouse address drop.
- **[The Hustle ~ First and Third Saturday Disco Party](https://dice.fm/event/bb7p6g-the-hustle-first-and-third-saturday-disco-party-4th-jul-gold-diggers-los-angeles-tickets)** — Gold Diggers, East Hollywood

## Friday · July 10

- **[Dances With Wolves](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3807?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz

- **[RE-MOLD. Night Market](https://posh.vip/e/remold-night-market-the-mold)** — The Mold · free
