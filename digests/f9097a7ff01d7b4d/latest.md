Quiet stretch on the sure-things front — a lot of the near-term Echo bills are unverified openers — but two real anchors bookend it: Alex Lahey this Friday, and further out, a Latin-alt double bill and an Osees desert run that are both too on-taste to leave off even though they push past three weekends.
