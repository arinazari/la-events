# Lori's LA Digest

*Digest regenerated Fri 7/10 — picks updated.*

Same story as the raw numbers always tell: the top of the score sheet is dominated by *Lost in Dreams*, an Insomniac dubstep/melodic-bass festival taking over LA State Historic Park all weekend (7/11–12) on "close to home" and editorial bumps, plus a wall of DTLA afterhours/bass-music nights riding the same mechanics. None of that is her lane, so it's filtered out below. What's left is the live-band, roots, and craft-pop stuff actually worth her night.

## Tonight — Friday 7/10

- **[Toody Cole & Her Band, OP ED](https://dice.fm/event/2w3v56-toody-cole-her-band-op-ed-10th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown, 7pm. Toody Cole co-founded Dead Moon and Pierced Arrows — raw, lo-fi Pacific Northwest garage-punk from a genuine lineage act, not a revival.

## This weekend — Sat 7/11 to Sun 7/12

- **[Lenny Kaye, Cindy Lee Berryhill](https://dice.fm/event/mx98nl-lenny-kaye-cindy-lee-berryhill-11th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Sat 7/11, Zebulon, 7pm. The pick of the run. Kaye is Patti Smith's longtime guitarist and the guy who compiled *Nuggets*, the garage-rock canon her whole Stooges end is built on — live, at her favorite room, next to singer-songwriter Cindy Lee Berryhill.
- **[Dave Alvin & Jimmie Dale Gilmore](https://www.mccabes.com/product/dave-alvin-jimmie-dale-gilmore-saturday-show/)** — Sat 7/11, McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica, 8pm. Two genuine legends sharing a bill — Alvin from The Blasters/X, Gilmore from The Flatlanders — American roots and outlaw country songwriting in the listening room built for exactly this. Friday's show already sold out; Saturday's still has tickets.
- **[Flying Lotus](https://www.ticketweb.com/event/flying-lotus-with-live-band-blue-note-los-angeles-tickets/14908773)** — Sat 7/11, Blue Note LA, Hollywood, 5pm. Playing with a live band, not solo behind a laptop — Brainfeeder jazz-electronic fusion in a proper listening room, the exception her "electronic only if it's special" rule exists for.
- **[Club Cumbia](https://dice.fm/event/8eqwx2-club-cumbia-11th-jul-the-virgil-los-angeles-tickets)** — Sat 7/11, The Virgil, East Hollywood, 10pm. No-filter cumbia, straight down the diaspora/global core of her taste, if she's up for a later start.
- **[Grand Ole Echo](https://www.ticketmaster.com/grand-ole-echo-los-angeles-california-07-12-2026/event/0900646425B0A86B)** — Sun 7/12, The Echo patio, Echo Park, 5:30pm. Weekly outdoor country/Americana series — sunset patio, roots music, her favorite room. Repeats most Sundays this run (also 7/19, 7/26) if this date doesn't line up.
- Worth a look if she's out late: **[TÉKA — Brazilian Jazz · New Bossa](https://www.vibratogrilljazz.com/music/tka-brazilian-jazz-new-bossa-4)**, Sat 7/11, Vibrato Grill Jazz, Bel Air, 10:30pm — Brazilian vocalist Téka with a full jazz combo, squarely in the global/roots lane, just a later and further-flung start.

## Weekend after — Thu 7/16 to Sun 7/19

- **[CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso — Free Spirits World Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/ca7riel-paco-amoroso-free-spirits-world-los-angeles-california-07-16-2026/event/09006475293F370C)** — Thu 7/16, Greek Theatre, Los Feliz, 8pm. Argentine duo doing genre-collapsing Latin alt/hip-hop with real critical heat right now — outdoor, close to home, squarely the diaspora lane.
- **[Alex Lahey](https://dice.fm/event/l8dnal-alex-lahey-17th-jul-permanent-records-roadhouse-los-angeles-tickets)** — Fri 7/17, Permanent Records Roadhouse, Cypress Park, 9pm. Aussie power-pop/indie-rock with hooks and a wry point of view — the craft-pop lane, guitar-forward, at the record-store venue.
- **[Desert Daze presents The Big Micro w/ DakhaBrakha](https://www.ticketmaster.com/desert-daze-presents-the-big-micro-los-angeles-california-07-18-2026/event/0900647B1DB0BE0F)** — Sat 7/18, The Bellwether, DTLA, 8pm. DakhaBrakha is a Ukrainian "ethno-chaos" folk quartet — this is the ethnomusicologist's spine of her taste in one booking, plus LA's own Brainstory playing live.
- **[Beats Antique](https://www.ticketmaster.com/beats-antique-los-angeles-california-07-17-2026/event/09006458DC2EF35E)** — Fri 7/17, Pacific Electric, DTLA, 8pm. World-fusion built from Middle Eastern and Balkan sources with a live band behind it — this is the ethnomusicologist lane by design.
- **[Futurebirds — Far Out Country Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/futurebirds-far-out-country-tour-los-angeles-california-07-17-2026/event/0900645BCC4C1CCC)** — Fri 7/17, Teragram Ballroom, Westlake, 7pm. Georgia alt-country/roots-rock, all ages — another straight shot at her Americana side.
- Worth a look if she wants something stranger: **[Eartheater](https://www.ticketweb.com/event/eartheater-the-masonic-lodge-at-hollywood-tickets/14915853)** at the Masonic Lodge at Hollywood Forever (Fri 7/17, also 7/15–16) — leftfield experimental/synthpop-industrial, a genuinely singular artist rather than a generic electronic booking.

## Weekend after that — Thu 7/23 to Sun 7/26

- **[Suki Waterhouse](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7Pe_N)** — Thu 7/23, Hollywood Forever Cemetery, 7:30pm. Confessional craft-pop with a real songwriting streak, in an outdoor setting she tends to like.
- **[Widespread Panic](https://www.ticketmaster.com/widespread-panic-los-angeles-california-07-24-2026/event/0900646CA6AD9272)** — Fri 7/24 & [Sat 7/25](https://www.ticketmaster.com/widespread-panic-los-angeles-california-07-25-2026/event/0900646CA6B0927E), Orpheum Theatre, DTLA, 8pm. Two-night stand from the veteran Southern jam band — classic-rock lineage and real improvisation in a proper theater.
- **[El Rayo-X](https://www.mccabes.com/product/el-rayo-x/)** — Fri 7/24, McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica, 8pm. David Lindley's old roots/world-fusion outfit at the legendary guitar-shop listening room — folk, blues, and international strains in one band; a longer drive from Glendale but exactly what McCabe's exists for.
- **[KCRW Presents: Emperor X w/ clovers daughter](https://www.ticketmaster.com/kcrw-presents-emperor-x-los-angeles-california-07-25-2026/event/0900648FBB408D7A)** — Sat 7/25, The Echo, Echo Park, 7pm. Lo-fi indie-folk with real lyrical teeth, carrying the KCRW editorial stamp she trusts.
- **[Bass Drum of Death, MEMO PST](https://dice.fm/event/xea8qk-bass-drum-of-death-memo-pst-25th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Sat 7/25, Zebulon, 7:30pm. Fuzzed-out Mississippi garage rock — straight down the Stooges-to-Osees end of her range, back at Zebulon.
- **[Teddy Thompson](https://www.mccabes.com/product/teddy-thompson/)** — Sun 7/26, McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica, 8pm. Folk singer-songwriter son of Richard & Linda Thompson, carrying the British-folk-into-Americana line — another proper listening-room close from a legitimate lineage act.
