# Lori's LA Digest — Aug 3 through late August

*Digest regenerated Mon 8/3 — picks updated.*

The headline this pass: Friday 8/7 is loaded — Juan de Marcos and the actual Afro-Cuban All Stars downtown, Judy Collins and Bruce Cockburn at the Ford, Gipsy Kings at the Greek, Old 97's at the Teragram, all on the same night. But Tuesday's Meridian Brothers / Mexican Institute of Sound double bill and Saturday's Strange Case album release are the two to actually build around.

---

## Tonight & this week — Mon 8/3 to Thu 8/6

**Mon 8/3** — Death Cab for Cutie plays the Greek tonight (7:30pm) if you want something bigger; otherwise it's a quiet Monday.

**Tue 8/5**
- **[Meridian Brothers + Mexican Institute of Sound](https://ra.co/events/2497831)** — The Regent Theater, DTLA, 8pm. Two of the sharpest minds in Latin alternative on one stage — Eblis Álvarez's surreal, tape-warped cumbia and vallenato next to Camilo Lara's sample-heavy Mexico City funk. They've recorded a collaborative album together, so expect them to trade songs. Your top pick of the week.

**Wed 8/6**
- **Alejandro Aranda** (American Idol's Scarypoolparty) — The Echo, Echo Park. A solid songwriter bill in a room you already trust.

## This weekend — Fri 8/7 to Sun 8/9

Friday's the crowded night — five real options, pick one and let the rest go:
- **Juan De Marcos w/ Afro-Cuban All Stars** — The United Theater on Broadway, DTLA. Buena Vista Social Club's own architects doing Cuban son at a real theater — the ethnomusicologist-core pick of the whole week, and the score has no idea.
- **Judy Collins w/ Bruce Cockburn** — The Ford, Hollywood. Two folk lifers in the outdoor garden setting you love.
- **Gipsy Kings ft. Nicolas Reyes** — Greek Theatre, Los Feliz. Flamenco-rumba legends, not a tribute act — the flat scoring badly undersells this.
- **Old 97's** — Teragram Ballroom, Westlake. Dependable alt-country veterans in a room you trust.
- **rocknite.la** (J'cuuzi, Salt, 3371) — Gold Diggers, East Hollywood. Scrappy DIY bill, right lane, unproven names — the discovery-angle pick if you want one.

Saturday is simpler:
- **Strange Case with Windows & Eighth Ball** — The Echo, Echo Park, 7pm. Three strong-affinity names on one album-release bill at your favorite Echo Park room. Build the weekend around this one.

## Next two weekends

- **Thu 8/14 — Momma** — The Bellwether, DTLA. Loving Breeders/Pavement-via-Nirvana revival on Polyvinyl, real critical buzz behind it.
- **Fri 8/21 — In Your Pocket Presents: The Asteroid No. 4** — The Echo, Echo Park.
- **Fri 8/22 — Livingmore** — The Echo, Echo Park.
- **Thu 8/28 — Brad Mehldau: Ride Into The Sun (The Music of Elliott Smith)** — Orpheum Theatre, DTLA. Mehldau, Chris Thile and Blake Mills doing chamber reinterpretations — serious musicianship, not a tribute-band nostalgia trip.

**On the radar:** Olivia Rodrigo's own one-day festival, **Daisy Chain Fields**, lands Sat 8/29 at Great Park Live in Irvine — an all-women lineup (Chappell Roan, Doechii, Mitski, The Breeders, Bikini Kill, Garbage, Santigold + surprise guests Stevie Nicks, Karen O, Sarah McLachlan). Off the Eastside and a real drive, but squarely your lane on paper — worth it if you can make the trip.

**Passing on:** Poppy's Constantly Nowhere Tour (leans metalcore, not the goth/art-rock crossover it promises), the Olivia Rodrigo DJ dance party at the Regent (it's a themed party, not a show), and KCON (no lineup announced yet, and it's not really your scene anyway).
