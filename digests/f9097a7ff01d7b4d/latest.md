# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Thu 7/2 — picks updated.*

*Lori's next few weeks, ranked for you — proto-punk and psych at Zebulon, roots royalty at McCabe's, a Brazilian-jazz/cumbia weekend, and a genuinely special global bill (DakhaBrakha) headlining the back half. July 4th itself is mostly fireworks parties and electronic afters — nothing worth carving out for you, so it's skipped below.*

## Tonight · Thu July 2

- **[Gary Wilson, Escape-ism, Jack Name](https://dice.fm/event/yowmea-gary-wilson-escape-ism-jack-name-2nd-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 8pm
  Escape-ism is Ian Svenonius (Nation of Ulysses, The Make-Up) doing his lo-fi proto-punk agitator thing solo; Gary Wilson's the outsider-pop cult weirdo behind *You Think You Really Know Me*. Odd, great triple bill in your home venue.

## Fri July 3

- **[Levitation Room, Sol Societe, Hector Waluyo, Walkin' Love](https://dice.fm/event/g5qe76-levitation-room-sol-societe-hector-waluyo-walkin-love-3rd-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7pm
  Levitation Room headlining — fuzzed-out, Elephant 6-leaning LA psych-pop, squarely your garage/psych lane. (Their DJs take over the room after for a free late one if you want to stay.)
- **[Luke Carlsen and the Fresh Rhythm Band](https://www.vibratogrilljazz.com/music/cxlanytymzxbehps5jpsmkmfxt6epm)** — Vibrato Grill Jazz, Bel Air · 7 & 9:30pm shows
  Rising young jazz vocalist/bandleader in the listening-room slot that's basically built for you.

## Sun July 5

- **[Harry Dean Stanton 100th Birthday: FREE Screening of *Lucky* (2017)](https://dice.fm/event/xeo28a-harry-dean-stanton-100th-birthday-free-screening-of-lucky-2017-5th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 9pm

## Wed July 8

- **[Royel Otis w/ Ax and the Hatchetmen](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7xK0J)** — Greek Theatre, Los Feliz · 7:30pm
  Midweek, but worth the trip: Sydney duo doing jangly, hooky, very-online indie-rock (the "Murder on the Dancefloor" cover was theirs) — real band, real songs, exactly your craft-pop-with-a-POV lane.

## Fri July 10

- **[Dave Alvin & Jimmie Dale Gilmore](https://www.mccabes.com/product/dave-alvin-jimmie-dale-gilmore-friday-show/)** — McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica · 8pm
  This is the one to build the night around. Dave Alvin (The Blasters) and Jimmie Dale Gilmore (The Flatlanders, Lubbock's cosmic-country soul) trading songs in a seated listening room — as ethnomusicologist-core as this feed gets. Runs Saturday too if Friday doesn't work.
- **[Toody Cole & Her Band, OP ED](https://dice.fm/event/2w3v56-toody-cole-her-band-op-ed-10th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7pm
  Toody Cole of Dead Moon (and Pierced Arrows) — Portland DIY garage-punk royalty, still doing it analog and unpolished.
- **[Loose Album Release Show](https://dice.fm/event/3oxplx-loose-album-release-show-10th-jul-permanent-records-roadhouse-los-angeles-tickets)** — Permanent Records Roadhouse, Cypress Park · 6pm
  Album-release night at the Roadhouse if you want an earlier, closer-to-home alternative.

## Sat July 11

- **[Lenny Kaye (Patti Smith Band), Cindy Lee Berryhill](https://dice.fm/event/mx98nl-lenny-kaye-patti-smith-band-cindy-lee-berryhill-11th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7pm
  Don't-miss. Lenny Kaye has been Patti Smith's guitarist since day one and compiled *Nuggets* — the garage-rock anthology that basically invented the genre's canon. Cindy Lee Berryhill (the "anti-folk" tag's OG) opens.
- **[TÉKA — Brazilian Jazz • New Bossa](https://www.vibratogrilljazz.com/music/tka-brazilian-jazz-new-bossa-4)** — Vibrato Grill Jazz, Bel Air · 7 & 9:30pm shows
  Brazilian vocalist/guitarist Téka with a quartet (Bob Sheppard on sax) doing bossa nova and MPB — straight into your global/diaspora core.
- **[Club Cumbia](https://dice.fm/event/8eqwx2-club-cumbia-11th-jul-the-virgil-los-angeles-tickets)** — The Virgil, East Hollywood · 10pm
  Late-night cumbia dance floor if you're still out — good pairing with either of the above.
- Also Saturday: **[Dave Alvin & Jimmie Dale Gilmore, second night](https://www.mccabes.com/product/dave-alvin-jimmie-dale-gilmore-saturday-show/)** at McCabe's, same as Friday above.

## Sat July 18

- **[Desert Daze Presents: The Big Micro ft. DakhaBrakha, Brainstory, Li Daiguo, DJ Nuge](https://www.ticketmaster.com/desert-daze-presents-the-big-micro-los-angeles-california-07-18-2026/event/0900647B1DB0BE0F)** — The Bellwether, DTLA · 8pm
  The pick of the next three weeks. DakhaBrakha are Kyiv's "ethno-chaos" quartet — tall wool hats, drone and percussion under fierce Ukrainian village-song harmonies, one of the genuinely great touring world-music live acts right now. Brainstory (Inland Empire psych-soul) and throat-singer/composer Li Daiguo round out an unusually well-curated global bill from Desert Daze's clubhouse series. Build the weekend around this one.
