# Lori's LA Events Digest
*Updated Tue 7/7 · Taste: ethnomusicologist ear — garage/proto-punk/post-punk through craft pop, global & diaspora roots music, live bands over DJs · Glendale (East LA)*

---
Three solid weekends of exactly your lane: garage-punk lineage at Zebulon two weekends running, a cumbia night, and a McCabe's roots-music booking that's basically what "ethnomusicologist" means in concert form. Heads up — the raw scoring pool this cycle is cluttered with bass/dubstep raves (Palladium, Academy, Exchange) tied at the same numeric score as these picks; they're not really your speed and I've filtered past them below.

## Top Picks

### Friday 7/10
**[Toody Cole & Her Band, OP ED](https://dice.fm/event/2w3v56-toody-cole-her-band-op-ed-10th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7pm
Toody Cole is half of Dead Moon — raw, lo-fi Pacific Northwest garage-punk royalty — in the room you already love.

### Saturday 7/11
**[Lenny Kaye (Patti Smith Band), Cindy Lee Berryhill](https://dice.fm/event/mx98nl-lenny-kaye-patti-smith-band-cindy-lee-berryhill-11th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7pm
Kaye played guitar in Patti Smith's band and literally curated *Nuggets*, the garage-punk comp that invented the genre you're into — this is the lineage, live, at Zebulon.

**[Club Cumbia](https://dice.fm/event/8eqwx2-club-cumbia-11th-jul-the-virgil-los-angeles-tickets)** — The Virgil, East Hollywood · 10pm
Straight cumbia, no filter — the diaspora/global core of your taste.

### Sunday 7/12
**[Grand Ole Echo](https://www.ticketmaster.com/grand-ole-echo-los-angeles-california-07-12-2026/event/0900646425B0A86B)** — The Echo, Echo Park · 5:30pm (patio)
Weekly outdoor country/Americana series — sunset setting, roots music, your favorite room. Repeats most Sundays through the summer (7/19, 7/26, 8/2…) if this one doesn't work.

### Friday 7/17
**[Alex Lahey](https://dice.fm/event/l8dnal-alex-lahey-17th-jul-permanent-records-roadhouse-los-angeles-tickets)** — Permanent Records Roadhouse, Cypress Park · 9pm
Hooky, sharp-witted Australian indie-rock songwriter — craft pop with real teeth, in the Mitski/Rodrigo lane but louder and more guitar-forward.

### Friday 7/24
**[El Rayo-X](https://www.mccabes.com/concerts-landing/)** — McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica · 8pm
David Lindley's old outfit — desert-tinged roots rock in the quintessential intimate listening room. It's a schlep from Glendale but this is exactly the kind of booking McCabe's exists for.

### Saturday 7/25
**[KCRW Presents: Emperor X with clovers daughter](https://www.ticketmaster.com/kcrw-presents-emperor-x-los-angeles-california-07-25-2026/event/0900648FBB408D7A)** — The Echo, Echo Park · 7pm
KCRW's own editorial pick — DIY indie songwriting with a point of view, the KCRW-mention boost doing its job.

**[Bass Drum of Death, MEMO PST](https://dice.fm/event/xea8qk-bass-drum-of-death-memo-pst-25th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · 7:30pm
Fuzzed-out, riff-first garage rock — third Zebulon garage booking this run, and worth the repeat visit.
