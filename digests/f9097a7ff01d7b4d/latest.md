# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sat 7/4 — picks updated.*

*Lori's next few weeks, ranked for you — a legit run of garage/punk lineage (Toody Cole of Dead Moon, Lenny Kaye of the Patti Smith Band), a UMO detour, Brazilian samba and bossa, Swamp Dogg's soul, DakhaBrakha's global chaos, and craft-pop from Alex Lahey and Suki Waterhouse — mostly in the small rooms she already loves.*

## Saturday · July 4

- **[Beach Boys](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7rkO0)** — Hollywood Bowl, Hollywood
  Nowhere near your usual lane, but it's the Beach Boys at the Bowl on the actual Fourth — a genuine LA holiday tradition at a venue you already love.
- **[Midnight Lovers with Eli Escobar (Day & Night)](https://ra.co/events/2415277)** — The Bridge, DTLA · $22 pre
  A genuine NYC disco-house veteran playing it warm and record-collection-deep rather than peak-time — the vinyl-groove pick if you don't want to chase a TBA warehouse address tonight.

## Sunday · July 5

- **[Townhouse Presents: Rogê Samba do Balde](https://dice.fm/event/nv5398-townhouse-presents-rog-samba-do-balde-5th-jul-townhouse-venice-venice-beach-tickets)** — Del Monte Speakeasy, Venice
  Brazilian samba in a Venice speakeasy — straight down the diaspora lane you live for.
- **[Jazz Sunday: Corbin Jones' Big Band plays Stevie Wonder's Hotter Than July](https://dice.fm/event/pyd7wp-jazz-sunday-corbin-jones-big-band-stevie-wonder-hotter-than-july-kevin-kanner-jazz-5th-jul-the-mint-los-angeles-tickets)** — The Mint, Mid-City
  A real jazz big band running the whole "Hotter Than July" album front to back — soul in a listening room on a Sunday night.

## Tuesday · July 7

- **[Jared Mattson & Ruban Nielson, Joshua Crumbley](https://dice.fm/event/eo8b7o-jared-mattson-ruban-nielson-joshua-crumbley-7th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown
  Ruban Nielson is Unknown Mortal Orchestra's frontman — a small-room detour from the day job that's exactly Zebulon's speed.

## Thursday · July 9

- **[Dylan LeBlanc + Band — 10 Year Anniversary of "Cautionary Tale"](https://dice.fm/event/bb7anv-dylan-leblanc-band-10-year-anniversary-of-cautionary-tale-9th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown
  A decade on from his breakout Americana record, playing it start to finish in the room he belongs in.

## Friday · July 10

- **[Loose Album Release Show](https://dice.fm/event/3oxplx-loose-album-release-show-10th-jul-permanent-records-roadhouse-los-angeles-tickets)** — Permanent Records Roadhouse, Cypress Park
  An album-release night at the Roadhouse is exactly the kind of intimate milestone show worth catching on faith, even with next to nothing else known about "Loose."
- **[Toody Cole & Her Band, OP ED](https://dice.fm/event/2w3v56-toody-cole-her-band-op-ed-10th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown
  Toody Cole co-founded Dead Moon and Pierced Arrows — unimpeachable garage-punk lineage, exactly the Stooges end of your range.

## Saturday · July 11

- **[Dave Alvin & Jimmie Dale Gilmore](https://www.mccabes.com/concerts-landing/)** — McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica
  Two roots-Americana legends sharing a stage in the city's best small listening room — the Friday show already sold out, so don't sleep on this one.
- **[TÉKA — Brazilian Jazz · New Bossa](https://www.vibratogrilljazz.com/music)** — Vibrato Grill Jazz, Bel Air
  A Brazilian vocalist leading a proper new-bossa combo in Herb Alpert's supper club — a direct hit on the diaspora/jazz spine.
- **[Swamp Dogg w/ Ben Vaughn Ensemble & DJ Eli 'Paperboy' Reed](https://thevenicewest.com/calendar)** — The Venice West, Venice
  A genuine soul legend at the old Witzend space — the funk-and-soul lane built for nights like this.
- **[Lenny Kaye (Patti Smith Band), Cindy Lee Berryhill](https://dice.fm/event/mx98nl-lenny-kaye-patti-smith-band-cindy-lee-berryhill-11th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown
  Lenny Kaye compiled "Nuggets" and has played guitar in the Patti Smith Band since '74 — about as close to garage-rock royalty as Frogtown gets.

## Friday · July 17

- **[Alex Lahey](https://dice.fm/event/l8dnal-alex-lahey-17th-jul-permanent-records-roadhouse-los-angeles-tickets)** — Permanent Records Roadhouse, Cypress Park
  Sharp, hooky Australian power-pop in a small room — right in the craft-pop lane.
- **[Adam Spry, Adam James, The Pretty Flowers](https://dice.fm/event/6dle87--adam-spry-adam-james-the-pretty-flowers-17th-jul-gold-diggers-los-angeles-tickets)** — Gold Diggers, East Hollywood
  A real indie/indie-folk triple bill at Gold Diggers instead of the venue's usual house night — Adam Spry plays warm Shins/Fruit Bats-style guitar pop, and The Pretty Flowers are three albums into a well-regarded local run.

## Saturday · July 18

- **[Desert Daze Presents The Big Micro ft. DakhaBrakha w/ Brainstory](https://www.ticketmaster.com/desert-daze-presents-the-big-micro-los-angeles-california-07-18-2026/event/0900647B1DB0BE0F)** — The Bellwether, DTLA
  DakhaBrakha are Ukraine's "ethno chaos" quartet — full-bore traditional/global music, backed by Brainstory's jazz-inflected neo-soul.

## Sunday · July 19

- **[The Marvelous Marilyn Maye](https://www.ticketmaster.com/the-marvelous-marilyn-maye-grammy-awardnominee-los-angeles-california-07-19-2026/event/090064C4E97B4A08)** — The Renberg Theatre, Hollywood
  A Grammy-nominated jazz vocalist still working the Great American Songbook in her 90s — a genuine legend in a small room.

## Thursday · July 23

- **[Suki Waterhouse](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7Pe_N)** — Hollywood Forever Cemetery, Hollywood
  Craft-pop songwriting in the Mitski/Olivia Rodrigo lane, outdoors among the headstones.

## Friday · July 24

- **[Orion 224 with Cannon the Dealers & American Woman](https://www.ticketmaster.com/orion-224-with-cannon-the-dealers-los-angeles-california-07-24-2026/event/0900649DE2A156D5)** — The Moroccan Lounge, Arts District
  A touring Texas indie-rock double bill — Orion 224's melodic guitars against Cannon the Dealers' scrappier indie-sleaze — not a big enough name to build the night around, but a solid live-band lane pick if you're already in the Arts District.

## Saturday · July 25

- **[KCRW Presents: Emperor X](https://www.ticketmaster.com/kcrw-presents-emperor-x-los-angeles-california-07-25-2026/event/0900648FBB408D7A)** — The Echo, Echo Park

## Sunday · July 26

- **[Wu Lyf](https://www.ticketmaster.com/wu-lyf-los-angeles-california-07-26-2026/event/090064C3EA438AA6)** — The Echo, Echo Park
