# Lori's LA Events Digest
*Digest regenerated Tue 7/14 — picks updated.*

The Echo and Permanent Records Roadhouse are doing the heavy lifting the next three weekends: [Alex Lahey](https://dice.fm/event/l8dnal-alex-lahey-17th-jul-permanent-records-roadhouse-los-angeles-tickets) brings guitar-forward Australian indie-rock to PRR Friday 7/17, [moondoggy w/ Sun Casino](https://www.ticketmaster.com/moondoggy-with-sun-casino-los-angeles-california-07-18-2026/event/090064492D4ED292) picks up Echo's Saturday rock slot the same weekend, and [Emperor X w/ clovers daughter](https://www.ticketmaster.com/kcrw-presents-emperor-x-los-angeles-california-07-25-2026/event/0900648FBB408D7A) (KCRW Presents, Sat 7/25) and [Los Aptos](https://www.ticketmaster.com/los-aptos-los-angeles-california-08-01-2026/event/090064AD7ED3549C) (Sat 8/1) keep that same loved room going after. The real one to build a night around is [Meridian Brothers + Mexican Institute of Sound](https://www.ticketmaster.com/meridian-brothers-mexican-institute-of-sound-los-angeles-california-08-05-2026/event/0900648DEE27B236) at The Regent (Wed 8/5) — an actual Latin-alt double bill for the diaspora ear, not a maybe. Two more worth flagging: [Los Lobos](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/the-best-things-to-do-in-la-this-weekend) play a free outdoor set at Burton Chace Park Saturday 7/18 (roots-rock royalty, worth the Marina del Rey trek), and [Yoshiki](https://www.musiccenter.org/tickets-free-events/lease-events/yoshiki/)'s classical-crossover solo show at Disney Hall Thursday 7/16 is a genuine curiosity outside the usual lane. Honest caveat: outside the Meridian Brothers and Los Lobos bookings, most of this stretch — including [PBW: Joey Cash w/ Babe Dylan](https://www.ticketmaster.com/pbw-presents-joey-cash-w-babe-los-angeles-california-07-31-2026/event/090064E70FDDDE16) at The Echo (Fri 7/31) — is unverified discovery bets rather than known draws: right room, right format, unproven names.
