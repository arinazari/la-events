# Lori's LA Digest

This window's one real conflict lands early: Wednesday 8/5 pits two ⭐ picks against each other — Meridian Brothers and Mexican Institute of Sound's genre-scrambling Latin bill at the Regent versus ZZ Top and Cheap Trick's classic-rock double bill at the Bowl — and only the former shares real DNA with your Chicano Batman streak. The pick actually worth circling, though, is further out: The Breeders at the Palladium on 8/24, about as close to the noisy guitar lineage behind Osees and Ty Segall as any legacy act gets this stretch. Everything between is a steady weeknight drip through your own rooms — The Echo, Zebulon, Permanent Records Roadhouse — stacked mostly with unproven openers, so treat those as discovery bets rather than sure things. Skip the electronic day-party/afters run (EMBRZ, Midnight Lovers, Recollect Underground, the two FLOAT pool parties, Nightshift) since it's all DJ format, and skip the classic-rock legacy circuit (Mellencamp, the Black Crowes bill, the Guess Who, Al Green) plus the arena-pop/K-pop run (Benson Boone, KCON, Mamamoo) — big rooms, none of it your lane.
