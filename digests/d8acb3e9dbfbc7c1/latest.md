## Thursday 7/16
- **[Yoshiki – Scarlet Night](https://www.musiccenter.org/tickets-free-events/lease-events/yoshiki/)** — Walt Disney Concert Hall. X Japan's Yoshiki in classical-crossover mode, orchestral reworkings of his own rock material rather than a full band show — a rare thing to catch in a room like Disney Hall.

## Friday 7/17
- **[Eartheater](https://www.ticketweb.com/event/eartheater-the-masonic-lodge-at-hollywood-tickets/14915853)** — Masonic Lodge at Hollywood Forever Cemetery. Brooklyn experimental/avant-pop artist (Alexandra Drewchin, PAN Records), genre-collapsing production closer to Björk/Arca than any club DJ, and a genuinely singular live performer.

## Saturday 7/18
- **[The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring — 25th Anniversary Concert](https://www.axs.com/events/1259229/the-lord-of-the-rings-the-fellowship-of-the-ring-tickets)** — Peacock Theater. Live symphony scoring a full screening of the film (also playing 7/16 and 7/17).
- **[Rosemary's Baby](https://www.discoverlosangeles.com/things-to-do/the-best-things-to-do-in-la-this-weekend)** — Cinespia at Hollywood Forever Cemetery. Polanski's 1968 psychological-horror classic on the outdoor cemetery lawn screen, still genuinely unsettling.
- **[Love Long Beach Festival](https://ra.co/events/2386257)** — Shoreline Aquatic Park, Long Beach. A real house-music bill, not a generic festival dump: Doc Martin, Lee Burridge, Justin Martin, Radio Slave, DJ Minx.
