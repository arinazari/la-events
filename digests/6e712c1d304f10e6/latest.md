# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sun 7/5 — picks updated.*

*A quiet stretch for straight indie/jazz specifically — the pool this run skews warehouse house-and-techno plus the Vista's rep-cinema calendar, so the picks below lean toward the moodier, more textural bookings and the classic-film nights that fit the lane best.*

## Friday · July 10

- **[Great Quality Entertainment presents Township Rebellion with Trou and DENYC](https://ra.co/events/2474681)** — 1720, Arts District · $21 pre
  The real draw of the weekend: melodic, emotionally-pitched techno closer to Lane 8 than a warehouse hammer — go in for build and atmosphere, not peak-time.
- **[Cirra presents: Day Thief, Faited, and sosoftly](https://ra.co/events/2472986)** — TBA, Downtown Los Angeles · $20-50
  Cirra's booked two names out of the SF/Oakland queer-underground soundsystem scene rather than the usual DTLA regulars — a rawer, genuinely different flavor of afters.
- **[Recollect Underground: LA Riots, Beast, Jacz, Lavenge, Max Rush](https://www.instagram.com/p/DZl9KXzKRBX/)** — Los Globos, Silver Lake · $10 pre
  Los Globos 11pm-5am with a locally-rooted tech-house/deep-house bill — solid if you're already eastside, though the support slots are thin enough to make this good-not-essential.

## Saturday · July 11

- **[Chopper](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3839?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Andrew Dominik's pitch-black Aussie crime-comedy with Eric Bana as real-life Melbourne gangland figure "Chopper" Read — the film that made his career before Hollywood. Good excuse for a Los Feliz rep night.

## Saturday · July 18

- **[Information - All Vinyl Night w/ Taylor Shockley + Luke Mele + CHOÍCE](https://ra.co/events/2456585)** — TBA, Downtown LA
  A location-TBA all-vinyl night built around Luke Mele's NTS-honed Balearic/ambient/dub sensibility — quieter and more textural than a peak-time club night, worth chasing the address for.
- **[Fandango](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3845?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Kevin Reynolds' 1985 cult favorite — Kevin Costner's first lead role, five college friends on one last road trip — a warm, low-stakes, unpretentious pick for a Saturday.

## Friday · July 24

- **[Fight Without Jingi](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3850?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Kinji Fukasaku's "Battles Without Honor and Humanity" under its literal title — the documentary-style, ultra-violent yakuza classic that basically invented the jitsuroku genre. Bracing over comfort-watch.
