# Demo (indie + jazz)'s LA Events Digest
*Digest regenerated Mon 7/13 — picks updated.*

*Updated Mon 7/13 · Taste: indie/rock/post-punk/dream-pop bands + jazz, soul & funk listening rooms + rep cinema + comedy, lukewarm on big-room electronic*

Most of this pool is TBA-warehouse house/techno and pool parties, which isn't the lane — but dig past the top of the mechanical sort and there's a genuinely solid three weekends here: Troubadour and McCabe's both have real bills, Sam First has two jazz-room nights worth planning around, and the New Beverly/Vidiots/Vista rep-cinema slate is deep. Comedy is thin — nothing from your list (Mulaney, Bargatze, Notaro, Bamford, Buress) is booked in this window, so skipping rather than padding with club listings.

## Thu 7/16 – Sun 7/19
**[Larry Goldings Trio with David Piltch & Joe La Barbera](https://www.samfirstbar.com/events/larry-goldings-trio-with-david-piltch-joe-la-barbera)** — Sam First, Westchester · Fri 7/17
A real jazz-room booking: Goldings on organ/piano with a serious rhythm section in LA's best small listening room. This is the platonic ideal of your jazz category.

**[Low Cut Connie](https://www.troubadour.com/)** — Troubadour, WeHo · Fri 7/17, 7pm
Sweaty, piano-driven bar-band rock at a venue you love. Reliable Friday-night pick.

**[All That Jazz](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/all-that-jazz-7-17-26-400-pm/)** — Vidiots, Eagle Rock · Fri 7/17, 4pm
Fosse's fever-dream musical on a real screen, early enough to still make a second stop.

**[James McMurtry w/ Bettysoo](https://www.troubadour.com/)** — Troubadour, WeHo · Sun 7/19, 6pm
Wry, novelistic songwriting — exactly the singer-songwriter/Americana lane you're into, and a low-key way to close the weekend.

*Also around: **A Clockwork Orange** at New Beverly (Thu–Sat) and **The Odyssey** in 70mm running most nights at the Vista, if you want a default rep-cinema plan.*

## Fri 7/24 – Sun 7/26
**[Trainspotting](https://vidiotsfoundation.org/showtimes/trainspotting-7-24-26-415-pm/)** — Vidiots, Eagle Rock · Fri 7/24, 4:15pm
Boyle's 1996 heroin-chic classic on a real screen — worth it even as a rewatch, and it loosely doubles up with *Young Frankenstein* playing later at the same venue that night.

**[Jane Monheit Duo with Max Haymer](https://samfirstbar.com/events/jane-monheit-duo-with-max-haymer)** — Sam First, Westchester · Fri 7/24 & Sat 7/25, 7:30pm
Great American Songbook standards from a genuine jazz-vocal name, two nights running — pick whichever fits your weekend better.

**[El Rayo-X](https://www.mccabes.com/concerts-landing/)** — McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica · Fri 7/24, 8pm
Roots-rock/folk at your favorite intimate room, walkable from the Westside.

**[Bass Drum of Death, MEMO PST](https://dice.fm/event/xea8qk-bass-drum-of-death-memo-pst-25th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · Sat 7/25, 7:30pm
Fuzzed-out garage rock at a venue you love — the closest thing to a straight indie bill this cycle.

*FYI: **Tom Rush** at McCabe's Sat 7/25 is already sold out — folk-legend-caliber booking, worth watching for a resale if you're a fan.*

## Fri 7/31 – Sun 8/2
**[12 Angry Men](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/sessions?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · Fri 7/31, 8pm
Lumet's jury-room classic — tight, tense, and a genuine credit to anyone's rep-cinema habit. Hard to go wrong with this one.

**[Andy McKee](https://www.mccabes.com/concerts-landing/)** — McCabe's Guitar Shop, Santa Monica · Fri 7/31, 8pm
Virtuoso fingerstyle guitar in the room built for exactly this.

**[Death Cab for Cutie: I Built You A Tower World Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/death-cab-for-cutie-i-built-los-angeles-california-08-02-2026/event/09006435E6A2D26C)** — Greek Theatre, Los Feliz · Sun 8/2
The obvious big-room indie pick to close out the run — Nation of Language opens.

**[Tortoise, Tara Jane O'Neil](https://dice.fm/event/bb23xo-tortoise-tara-jane-oneil-2nd-aug-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown · Sun 8/2
If you'd rather go small and weird than big and loud that same night: Chicago post-rock legends in a room that seats/stands maybe 200.
