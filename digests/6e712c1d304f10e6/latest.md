# LA Events
*Digest regenerated Sat 7/4 — picks updated.*

*The next three weekends, ranked for indie/rock, jazz, and rep-cinema — the Vista's run of classics is doing a lot of the work this stretch, with a few DTLA electronic nights that clear the melodic/live bar and one certified Ruban Nielson curveball on a Tuesday.*

## Saturday · July 4

- **[The Hustle ~ First and Third Saturday Disco Party!](https://dice.fm/event/bb7p6g-the-hustle-first-and-third-saturday-disco-party-4th-jul-gold-diggers-los-angeles-tickets)** — Gold Diggers, East Hollywood
  Gold Diggers' reliable disco-and-boogie residency, no headliner, just the residents doing their thing. Easy, unpretentious Fourth-of-July add.
- **[Jayda G](https://dice.fm/event/k6dyll-jayda-g-4th-jul-sound-nightclub-los-angeles-tickets)** — Sound Nightclub, Hollywood · $31
  Sound is a proper room for her — long, sweaty sets that pull the floor through disco into gospel-house without a hard stop.
- **[Jaws](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3669?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 11:30pm
  A late Vista showing of the original summer blockbuster — the no-brainer rep pick, always worth the midnight end time.

## Tuesday · July 7

- **[Jared Mattson & Ruban Nielson, Joshua Crumbley](https://dice.fm/event/eo8b7o-jared-mattson-ruban-nielson-joshua-crumbley-7th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets)** — Zebulon, Frogtown
  Unknown Mortal Orchestra's Ruban Nielson and Jared Mattson (of the Mattson 2) in a small Frogtown room — a loose, jazz-fusion-leaning side of his playing that doesn't make it onto a UMO record. Worth breaking the weekend-only pattern for.

## Friday · July 10

- **[Dances With Wolves](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3807?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz · 8pm
  Costner's three-hour 1990 epic on the Vista's big screen and vintage marquee — a genuinely good way to spend a Friday, and the early showing skips a midnight drive home.
- **[Cirra presents: Day Thief, Faited, and sosoftly](https://ra.co/events/2472986)** — TBA, DTLA · $20-50
  Two names out of the SF/Oakland queer-underground soundsystem scene (Faited runs sound for Eris Drew's crew) instead of the usual DTLA regulars — a rawer flavor of afters.
- **[Great Quality Entertainment presents Township Rebellion with Trou and DENYC](https://ra.co/events/2474681)** — 1720, Arts District · $21 pre
  Township Rebellion's the real draw — melodic, emotionally-pitched techno closer to Lane 8 than a warehouse hammer. Trou's an unknown on the bill.

## Saturday · July 11

- **[Chopper](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3839?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Eric Bana as real-life Melbourne gangland figure Mark "Chopper" Read — pitch-black Aussie crime-comedy that made his career before Hollywood. Good Los Feliz double-feature bait.
- **[SMD - Mike Servito & Jason Kendig](https://ra.co/events/2477599)** — TBA, DTLA · $47 pre
  Servito and Kendig have logged years of Honcho and Honey Soundsystem nights together — they build a room slowly instead of peak-time-blasting it. TBA location fits the sound.

## Friday · July 17

- **[The Odyssey (70mm)](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3745?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Nolan's "The Odyssey" in true 70mm — a format event as much as a film one, and exactly the reason to see it at the Vista instead of streaming it.
- **[Starman](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/sessions?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Carpenter's underrated 1984 sci-fi romance with Jeff Bridges as a stranded alien — gentler and warmer than his horror run, a nice change of pace on the Vista's slate.

## Saturday · July 18

- **[Information - All Vinyl Night w/ Taylor Shockley + Luke Mele + CHOíCE](https://ra.co/events/2456585)** — TBA, DTLA
  Luke Mele's NTS-honed Balearic/ambient/dub sensibility on an all-vinyl bill — the listening-bar format worth chasing a TBA address for.
- **[Fandango](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/purchase/3845?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww)** — Vista Theater, Los Feliz
  Kevin Reynolds' 1985 cult favorite — Costner's first lead, five friends on one last road trip before the draft. Warm, funny, low-stakes Saturday.

## Friday · July 24

- **[Stranger Than presents: Stavroz (Live)](https://ra.co/events/2437239)** — The Bridge, DTLA
  They play live rather than DJ — closer to the Bonobo/RÜFÜS Du Sol live-electronic lane than a club night, warm and cinematic rather than peak-time. The melodic-organic pick without the warehouse chase.

## Saturday · July 25

- **[CSW - Nick León [All Night]](https://ra.co/events/2475085)** — TBA, DTLA
  An all-night set that isn't a straight house night — Miami dembow and dancehall woven into club music, one of the more distinctive bookings on the calendar.
