# Demo (indie + jazz)'s LA Events Digest
*Digest regenerated Wed 7/15 — picks updated.*

The pool is still stacked with DTLA warehouse afters and TBA-location club nights that just aren't this lane — but once you filter those out, three solid weekends of indie/rock, folk, and small-room jazz shake loose, most of them at rooms already on your list (Troubadour, McCabe's, Zebulon). Standing rec: the [Vista Theater's 70mm run of *The Odyssey*](https://ticketing.uswest.veezi.com/sessions?siteToken=20xhpa3yt2hhkwt4zjvfcwsaww) is playing nightly through this whole stretch — a real print-and-projector event, not something to wait and stream.

## Weekend of 7/17

**Day 7/17 (Fri)**
- [Low Cut Connie](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7x4uK) — Troubadour, 7pm. Bar-band rock and roll at a venue you love; easy Friday-night pick.
- [Larry Goldings Trio](https://www.samfirstbar.com/events/larry-goldings-trio-with-david-piltch-joe-la-barbera) (w/ David Piltch, Joe La Barbera) — Sam First, 7:30pm. Hammond B-3 jazz trio in the small-room format you're after.

**Day 7/18 (Sat)**
- [Desert Daze Presents The Big Micro: DakhaBrakha w/ Brainstory](https://www.ticketmaster.com/desert-daze-presents-the-big-micro-los-angeles-california-07-18-2026/event/0900647B1DB0BE0F) — The Bellwether, 8pm. Brainstory's jazz-inflected neo-soul opening set is the real draw here if the headliner's not your thing.

**Day 7/19 (Sun)**
- [James McMurtry w/ Bettysoo](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7x4bS) — Troubadour, 6pm. Americana/folk songwriting, another venue-you-love night.
- [In the Big Round](https://www.mccabes.com/product/in-the-big-round/) — McCabe's Guitar Shop, 8pm. Folk in the round at the most intimate room on your list.

## Weekend of 7/24

**Day 7/24 (Fri)**
- [El Rayo-X](https://www.mccabes.com/product/el-rayo-x/) — McCabe's Guitar Shop, 8pm.
- [Riff Wood w/ Cherry Bombs](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/Z7r9jZ1A7x_jv) — Troubadour, 7pm.
- [Brenna Whitaker](https://www.vibratogrilljazz.com/music/7n7gr38hgd84szrcfygxbmxz87tmgg) — Vibrato Grill Jazz, 11pm. Vintage jazz and soul vocals, late set.

**Day 7/25 (Sat)**
- [Tom Rush](https://www.mccabes.com/product/tom-rush/) — McCabe's Guitar Shop, 8pm. Folk-revival legend — already sold out, but worth knowing about for resale.
- [Bass Drum of Death, MEMO PST](https://dice.fm/event/xea8qk-bass-drum-of-death-memo-pst-25th-jul-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets) — Zebulon, 7:30pm. The best-scoring indie/rock pick of the whole pull, at a venue you love.

**Day 7/26 (Sun)**
- [Teddy Thompson](https://www.mccabes.com/product/teddy-thompson/) — McCabe's Guitar Shop, 8pm. More folk at the room built for it.

## Weekend of 7/31

**Day 7/31 (Fri)**
- [Andy McKee](https://www.mccabes.com/product/andy-mckee/) — McCabe's Guitar Shop, 8pm. Fingerstyle guitar virtuoso.
- [Violent Femmes: 45 Years Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/violent-femmes-45-years-of-violent-los-angeles-california-07-31-2026/event/09006490BF5797D2) — The Bellwether, 7pm. Nostalgia lap, not a discovery night, but it delivers exactly what it says if you grew up on this record.

**Day 8/1 (Sat)**
- [Kim Gordon: Play Me Tour](https://www.ticketmaster.com/kim-gordon-play-me-tour-los-angeles-california-08-01-2026/event/09006482BE637FB6) (w/ SASAMI) — Pacific Electric, 8pm. Sonic Youth's Kim Gordon gone weirder and more interesting solo, with SASAMI opening — a real bill, not a legacy cash-in. Top pick of this weekend.
- [of Montreal](https://www.ticketmaster.com/of-montreal-los-angeles-california-08-01-2026/event/09006470A5716795) (w/ Sloppy Jane) — The Regent Theater, 9pm.

**Day 8/2 (Sun)**
- [Tortoise, Tara Jane O'Neil](https://dice.fm/event/bb23xo-tortoise-tara-jane-oneil-2nd-aug-zebulon-los-angeles-tickets) — Zebulon, 8pm. Chicago post-rock royalty in a room the size of Zebulon, with Tara Jane O'Neil's hushed solo set opening — worth the drive to Frogtown.
