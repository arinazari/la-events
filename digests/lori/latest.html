discostan
El Cid, Silver Lake · $11.50-17.25
Discostan at El Cid is a rare thing — a DJ night genuinely organized around politics, diaspora, and crate-digging the broader Islamic-influenced musical world rather than genre categories. Worth going curious rather than expecting a straight house night. — discostan — LA collective (Arshia Haq + Jeremy Loudenback) founded in 2011 — crate-digging across the Arab world, the Subcontinent, Iran, North Africa and diaspora pop, framed as 'the imaginary migratory homeland.' Not a dance-music-as-usual proposition; vinyl-heavy global selection, community-first. Regularly on RA and documented in Mixmag as a key South Asian diaspora dance collective.
