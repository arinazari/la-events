# LA Events — Weekend of Fri 6/19–Sun 6/21

Fresh pull this morning, and it's a loaded one: **Pride weekend**, with **Juneteenth** on
Friday and **Summer Solstice + Father's Day** closing it out Sunday — plus the **World Cup**
is in town (see Around town). ~300 events in the window, filtered hard to the house/techno/
rooftop lane with the live-band rooms, rep cinema, and the Pride tentpoles folded in. The
new bit this run: **DICE is finally being captured**, so Zebulon, Gold Diggers and 2220 Arts
all show up properly now.

## Don't-miss
- **[Midnight Lovers Day Party w/ Bradley Zero](https://ra.co/events/2415278)** — Sat 6/20,
  The Bridge (DTLA), 5pm. Rhythm Section's Peckham boss doing sun-down disco-leaning house —
  about as close to the Sunset Sessions feeling as the weekend gets. Build Saturday around it.
- **[we own the night — 15-yr anniversary w/ Antal](https://ra.co/events/2409967)** — Sat 6/20,
  DTLA warehouse (TBA). They fly in Rush Hour's Antal (Amsterdam, one of the deepest diggers
  alive) for an extended set. No velvet ropes, dirty-disco-into-house — worth chasing a TBA address.
- **[Chris Lake](https://ra.co/events/2367331)** (+ Hot Since 82) — Fri + Sat, LA State Historic
  Park. The festival-scale option; **Saturday's the one** — Hot Since 82 gives the open-air a
  deeper European backbone. $105+, going fast.
- **[Spirits: Summer Solstice Dance w/ Richie Hawtin](https://ra.co/events/2458874)** — Sun 6/21,
  LACMA, *free w/ RSVP*. Plastikman in a museum-plaza art context (paired with John Gerrard's
  *SPIRITS* piece) — textural and hypnotic rather than a peak-time hammer. The Sunday move.
- **[Midnight Lovers w/ Eddie C — open to close](https://ra.co/events/2420856)** — Fri 6/19,
  Bar Franca, *free w/ RSVP*. One of the slo-mo disco crowd's best diggers (Endless Flight)
  building the room all night, no warm-up, no grandstanding. A tracked name and a no-brainer.
- **📌 [Sunset Sessions](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/sunset-sessions-at-golden-hour-dtla-a-weekly-series-tickets-1976818512385)**
  — your rooftop-vinyl-house pick at Level 8, on *both* nights: golden-hour
  [Fri](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/sunset-sessions-at-golden-hour-dtla-a-weekly-series-tickets-1976818512385)
  (free w/ RSVP) and a [Sat pool party](https://ra.co/events/2468953). Local residents, but the
  setting and sound are the whole point.
