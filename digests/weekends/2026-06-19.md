# LA Events — Weekend of Fri 6/19–Sun 6/21

A loaded one: **Pride weekend**, with **Juneteenth** Friday and **Summer Solstice +
Father's Day** Sunday. ~300 events in the window, so this is filtered hard to the
house/techno/rooftop lane, with the live-band rooms, rep cinema, and a couple of Pride
tentpoles folded in.
